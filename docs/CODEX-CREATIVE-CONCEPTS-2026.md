# OMRAN 2026 — Codex Creative Direction & Execution Brief

**Status:** concept exploration and review only. **No public site edits or deployment are authorized by this brief.**

## 1. Objective

Act as a senior creative director, visual communication specialist and e-commerce UX lead for the existing **شركة عمران التجارية / OMRAN Toys & Gifts** storefront at https://omrantoys.store. Design five genuinely distinct **visual systems** that elevate product discovery, brand trust and WhatsApp enquiry conversion, using the authentic current identity, not a rebrand.

Primary audiences: Tanta/Gharbia families, parents buying toys and gifts, occasion shoppers, and wholesale buyers (whose separate purchasing journey must not expose wholesale prices publicly).

Relevant event: planned El-Sayed El-Badawi branch opening **26 November 2026**, at Midan El-Sayed El-Badawi, Darb El-Abshihi Street, Tanta. Secondary branch: Stadium area, opposite City Club and Si El-Sayed Restaurant, Tanta. Verify details before publication.

Messaging anchor (existing brand docs): **«اختيارات أكثر. اختيار أسهل. خدمة تثق فيها.»** Alternatives are proposals only until owner-approved. Primary public enquiry CTA: **«اسأل عن السعر والتوفر»** via WhatsApp, consistent with existing runtime policy.

## 3. Creative system: five candidate directions

These are **starting hypotheses**, not approved designs. Validate their fit against real page screenshots, current assets, and customer tasks, and improve them as needed.

### Concept 01 — عالم عمران | OMRAN World

- **Metaphor / idea:** a coherent collection of 'discovery windows': each real product category opens a new possibility, not a generic cartoon universe.
- **Art direction:** energetic but refined editorial retail; clean background, controlled geometric shapes, restrained yellow/red highlights.
- **Composition:** mobile-first one featured verified product, small supporting category tiles, short Arabic headline, one primary CTA. Product > message > action > secondary choice.
- **Colors / type:** production blue/navy with soft neutral surfaces and small yellow accents; Cairo Bold title, Cairo Regular details.
- **Imagery:** isolated genuine products and approved category photos with consistent camera angles; subtle arcs/frame systems, no unlicensed characters.
- **Emotion / commercial rationale:** curiosity and playful exploration without visual overload; improve category discovery and intent.
- **Best placements:** homepage discovery, category graphics, Reels end cards. **Risk:** clutter; cap decorative elements and avoid multiple CTAs.

### Concept 02 — عمران سيليكت | OMRAN Select

- **Metaphor / idea:** trusted curated showroom; the true product is the visual centerpiece.
- **Art direction:** restrained premium product studio, natural shadows, honest textures and neutral backdrops; avoid fake luxury or misleading scale.
- **Composition:** 60–70% of social canvas for product, compact name/benefit, original logo small, one WhatsApp CTA. Square cards and 4:5 social variants.
- **Colors / type:** white/`#F7F9FC` dominant, navy type, blue functional actions; Cairo 700 for names and 400–600 supporting copy.
- **Imagery:** actual product shots, clean cutouts only if they preserve shape and packaging; close-ups as verified secondary assets.
- **Emotion / commercial rationale:** clarity, quality, confidence; raise comprehension and product enquiry.
- **Best placements:** product cards/details, listing thumbnails, social product spotlight. **Risk:** too clinical; add restrained brand motifs and human benefit language.

### Concept 03 — فرحة كل بيت | Family Moments

- **Metaphor / idea:** the moment of choosing and giving a gift; family benefit without pretending staged imagery shows a sold item.
- **Art direction:** candid warm lifestyle, human-scale framing, calm natural lighting, local Egyptian context when verified or commissioned.
- **Composition:** truthful real-product close-up first; lifestyle secondary; emotional one-line headline, one enquiry CTA; mobile text never overlaps faces/products.
- **Colors / type:** production navy/blue structure with warm, subtle yellow; Cairo readable RTL hierarchy.
- **Imagery:** commissioned authentic family/product photos with permissions; avoid depicting a model using a different product or making unverified age-safety implications.
- **Emotion / commercial rationale:** connection, reassurance, gifting inspiration; help parents choose.
- **Best placements:** gift finder, family campaigns, category landing stories. **Risk:** generic lifestyle photography; require asset provenance and real product match.

### Concept 04 — عمران يجمعنا | OMRAN Together

- **Metaphor / idea:** the physical shopping journey ends in a shared celebration of the verified branch opening; connect local trust to the store.
- **Art direction:** contemporary celebration, branch storefront photographs when available, elegant signage/wayfinding with clean festive accents; not an uncontrolled balloon explosion.
- **Composition:** opening name/date/location clearly prioritized; actual storefront or verified environment; prominent directions/contact action; ensure logo stays intact.
- **Colors / type:** navy/blue for trust, yellow for warmth, red minimal accent; Cairo Bold headline and spacious location details.
- **Imagery:** real branch photos, approved opening visuals, legible street/branch directions. Avoid inventing prizes, giveaways or opening offers.
- **Emotion / commercial rationale:** excitement, belonging and confidence to visit.
- **Best placements:** launch campaign, social countdown, storefront posters and printed assets. **Risk:** unverified logistics; obtain owner sign-off on date, branch details, offers and image rights before release.

### Concept 05 — اختار بسهولة | Smart Choice

- **Metaphor / idea:** a visual decision path from need to suitable real products—age, category, occasion and gifting budget *when verified data is available*.
- **Art direction:** clean structured commerce, strong information design, straightforward hierarchy and clear category iconography.
- **Composition:** search and category-first on mobile, meaningful chips and real product image grid; clear WhatsApp-assisted choice CTA.
- **Colors / type:** blue interactive tokens, navy headings, neutral backgrounds, restrained functional accents; Cairo 400/600/700.
- **Imagery:** verified product thumbnails, icons only where categories/attributes are actually supported by catalog data.
- **Emotion / commercial rationale:** control and ease; reduce hesitation and wrong enquiries.
- **Best placements:** mobile homepage, navigation, category directory, gift-finder entry. **Risk:** false product metadata; do not invent ages/availability/filters.

## 4. Required Codex deliverables — phase A (planning ONLY)

1. **Read-only baseline audit:** current page map, relevant UI component references, confirmed branding/asset availability, mobile UX and SEO/performance/accessibility observations. Separate **verified**, **inferred**, **unknown**.
2. **Five concept sheets**, each with: creative metaphor, audience/problem, art direction, hierarchy/composition, color/typography, genuine imagery requirements, emotion, CTA, placement, reuse rules, risks and why the visual idea supports measurable store goals.
3. **Comparison matrix** scoring each candidate /100: uniqueness 20, identity fit 20, commercial impact 25, implementation ease 15, scalability 20. Scores should be evidence-based estimates, not measured conversion uplift.
4. **Recommendation:** favor the strongest coherent product-display foundation plus one complementary campaigns system, explaining tradeoffs and what **not** to mix.
5. **Three implementation specs, no production changes:** (a) responsive homepage hero, (b) authentic product social/website feature, (c) El-Sayed El-Badawi opening announcement. For each state required real assets, Arabic copy proposal, CTA, mobile/desktop ratio, safe areas and approval needs.
6. **Decision log:** owner approvals needed, missing photos/product IDs, conflicting legacy docs or unsupported business claims, and a small phased future implementation plan.
7. Put review-only deliverables in `docs/creative/OMRAN-CREATIVE-REVIEW-2026.md` in a **separate Codex PR**, without changes to `client/`, `public/catalog/`, `functions/`, `wrangler`, or deploy workflows.
