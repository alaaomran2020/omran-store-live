# Omran VIP Card Program — MVP PRD

Status: Draft for pilot approval.

## Outcome

Sell and operate two controlled card types, Omran VIP and Omran Silver, while proving unit economics, partner compliance and repeat visits without mixing Omran Toys and POP UP catalogs or identities.

## Pilot scope

- Tanta, with launch priority for El-Sayed El-Badawy branch, then Stadium branch.
- 5–10 documented partners.
- Configurable one-to-three-month pilot period.
- Phone registration, unique membership and card IDs, manual serial/QR lookup and card lifecycle. Real-time secure QR verification is out of scope while API and token use are prohibited.
- Offer rules with minimum invoice, maximum discount, usage limit, exclusions, dates, funding owner and offer budget.
- Google-account-restricted pilot operations for issuance, activation, redemption, replacement and complaints. Partner self-service remains gated pending an authenticated transaction layer.
- Points inside Omran Toys only, disabled until the points policy and liability are approved.
- Public `/vip` page with no unapproved prices, partner claims or discount promises.

## Non-goals for MVP

- A standalone mobile app.
- A new custom server, VPS or paid SaaS.
- Partner points settlement.
- Automated paid raffle or purchase-conditioned draw.
- Publishing unapproved card prices, discounts, partners or contact details.

## Acceptance gates

1. Commercial settings approved and financial model reviewed.
2. Partner agreement signed and offer record marked approved.
3. Consumer terms and privacy controls reviewed by Egyptian counsel.
4. Atomic redemption and replacement tests pass.
5. Printed QR scan succeeds on representative phones and store lighting.
6. Staff and partner operating tests pass using test cards only.
7. No regression in storefront, WhatsApp flow or POP UP separation.
8. No browser-shipped secret or direct unauthenticated financial write endpoint.

## Measures

Track cards issued, paid and activated separately; card revenue; direct issuance and acquisition cost; Omran-funded maximum liability; redemptions; unique customers; repeat purchase; partner usage; complaints and resolution time; points earned, redeemed and expired; and contribution after realized and reserved benefit costs.
