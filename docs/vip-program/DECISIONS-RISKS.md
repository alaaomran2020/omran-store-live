## Legal review gate

The program must be reviewed against Egypt Consumer Protection Law No. 181/2018 and its Executive Regulations No. 822/2019. Personal data processing must also be reviewed against Law No. 151/2020 and Executive Regulations No. 816/2025, including consent records, purpose limitation, retention, security, data-subject requests, licensing/permit requirements and any cross-border processing. Promotional draws require a separate written opinion on the exact entry mechanism and permits before announcement. This register is not legal approval.
